## 1. The service

1.1 Lyra Pet provides software for running a pet grooming business, and a marketplace where pet owners ("**Customers**") can find your salon and send booking requests.

1.2 Using Lyra Pet is free. There is no subscription or setup fee. We earn a service fee only on bookings paid through the app (clause 4).

1.3 You provide the grooming services, and you alone are responsible for them, for your staff, and for your dealings with Customers. Lyra Pet is not a party to the contract between you and a Customer.

## 2. Your account

2.1 You must give accurate business details, including your ABN and GST status, and keep them up to date.

2.2 You are responsible for everyone you invite to your account and for what they do in it. Give staff only the access they need.

## 3. Online payments

3.1 **Stripe.** To take payments through Lyra Pet, you must open and keep a Stripe account through our Stripe Connect onboarding and accept Stripe's terms ([Stripe Connected Account Agreement](https://stripe.com/au/legal/connect-account)). Stripe, not Lyra Pet, processes payments and pays out to your bank account.

3.2 **Merchant of record.** Payments are made to your Stripe account in your business name. You are the seller and the merchant of record. You are responsible for your prices, the tax invoice or receipt you give the Customer, and for GST on your services.

3.3 **Payment in full at booking.** If you take online payments, Customers pay the full estimated price shown on your listing when they send a request. The request reaches you only after payment succeeds.

3.4 **Price changes.** If the final price at the appointment differs from the amount paid (for example, extra time for a matted coat), you settle the difference with the Customer directly. Lyra Pet's fee is not charged on amounts settled outside the app.

3.5 **Stripe's fees.** Stripe charges its card processing fees to your Stripe account. These are separate from Lyra Pet's service fee.

## 4. Lyra Pet service fee

4.1 For each booking a Customer pays through Lyra Pet, you pay a service fee of **5% of the amount paid, including GST**.

4.2 The fee is deducted automatically from each payment, as an application fee through Stripe, at the time of payment. You receive the payment less the service fee and Stripe's fees.

4.3 **Refunds.** If a paid booking is refunded in full because:
- you decline the request,
- the request expires before it is accepted, or
- the Customer cancels before the appointment in line with your cancellation policy,

then the service fee is returned to you in full.

4.4 **No fee on bookings outside the app.** There is no fee on appointments you create yourself, or on bookings paid at your salon.

4.5 **Tax invoices.** Each month we provide a tax invoice for the service fees in Settings → Payments, with your statement of payments and refunds.

4.6 **Changes to the fee.** We may change the fee by giving you at least 30 days' notice. If you don't agree, you can stop taking online payments or close your account before the change takes effect.

## 5. Refunds, cancellations and disputes

5.1 Lyra Pet automatically refunds the Customer in full if a paid request is declined, expires, or is cancelled by the Customer where your cancellation policy allows it (before confirmation, or before your cancellation window starts).

5.2 You must honour the cancellation policy shown on your listing. You must also give Customers the remedies they are entitled to under the Australian Consumer Law, which cannot be excluded.

5.3 You are responsible for chargebacks and disputes on payments to your Stripe account, as set out in Stripe's terms. Where a refund or chargeback relates to a booking, the corresponding service fee is returned.

## 6. Your obligations

6.1 You will keep your listing accurate, including services, prices, hours and policies. You will respond to requests promptly.

6.2 You will comply with applicable laws, including the Australian Consumer Law, the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) where it applies to you, animal welfare laws, and the Spam Act 2003 (Cth) for marketing messages.

6.3 You won't use Lyra Pet to take bookings off the platform to avoid the service fee for Customers who found you through the Lyra Pet marketplace. [Optional; consider whether to include.]

7.1 You own your business data. We process it to provide the service, in line with our Privacy Policy.

7.2 Customer contact details are shared with you only for bookings with your salon.

## 8. Liability

8.1 Nothing in these terms excludes rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law that cannot be excluded.

8.2 Subject to clause 8.1, our total liability to you in any 12 months is limited to the service fees you paid us in that period. We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss.

## 9. Ending the agreement

9.1 You can close your account at any time. Bookings already paid will be completed or refunded.

9.2 We may suspend or close an account that breaches these terms, with notice where reasonable.
